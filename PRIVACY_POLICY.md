#### Information Collected while Using the Application
While using Our Application, in order to provide features of Our Application, We may collect, with Your prior permission:
* **Information regarding your location:** We use Your location data (GPS coordinates) to provide environmental real-time UV Index alerts and localized weather forecasts for sunscreen alarms.
* **Pictures and other information from your Device's camera and photo library:** With Your prior permission, We access Your device's camera and photo gallery strictly when You choose to perform an AI Facial Scan or update Your user profile picture. Facial photos are processed solely to analyze skin features (such as acne conditions, oiliness levels, pore structure, and skin texture) in order to generate customized AI skincare recommendations and track Your skin progress over time. We do not use Your facial images for biometric identification, facial recognition surveillance, marketing, or unauthorized third-party tracking.

We use this information to provide features of Our Service, to improve and customize Our Service. The information may be uploaded to the Company's servers and/or a Service Provider's server or it may be simply stored on Your device.

You can enable or disable access to this information at any time, through Your Device settings.
